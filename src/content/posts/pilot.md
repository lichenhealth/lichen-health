**The Solution**

**A Comprehensive Model with Four Pillars**

1. Land-Based Regulation: Nature immersion, fire circles, water therapies, land stewardship. Provided primarily through Mons Sana's retreat facility, with ongoing connection to land as a therapeutic partner
2. Community-Based Healing: Pod cohorts of 8-12 individuals, shared meals, daily rituals, peer frameworks. Those who complete the program at Mons Sana become part of the ongoing healing community—because community-based healing for PTSD requires sustained connection, not episodic treatment
3. Self-Regulation & Awareness: Somatic modalities, clinical treatment, integrative trauma care. Heroes Journey Center provides ASAM-compliant detox, IOP, and residential treatment with culturally competent, veteran-specific protocols
4. Meaning & Purpose Reconstruction: Identity rebuilding, leadership tracks, alumni transition pathways. Warriors and first responders don't just recover—they become beacons of strength and wellness who often return as donors and peer supporters

**A Robust Measurement Protocol**

Assemble three groups of veterans and first responders with representative health profiles.

* Control Group: Receives the incumbent standard of care
* Group 1: Lichen + Heroes Journey Center
* Group 2: Lichen + Heroes Journey Center + Mons Sana

Compare health outcomes and care costs across the three groups over time to test our hypothesis that:

* Holistic, functional medicine is more effective than the incumbent standard of care
* Holistic, functional medicine is cheaper than the incumbent standard of care
* When Entheogenic medicines help us re-member that our mental, physical, spiritual, social, environmental and systemic health are inextricably intertwined, we unlock higher levels of self-healing and growth
