As a Progressive Web Application that is not hosted by any incumbent technology platforms, member data is protected within the network. Because members pay for the utility of the platform, they're not the product, so no outside data sharing is necessary to fuel advertising or marketing.

While Artificial Intelligence is only used to optimize the sharing of resources and information in pursuit of individual, social and ecological healing and growth, members can opt out of the feature if they choose.

Limited integrations are made available to bridge to outside currency systems, commerce platforms and utilities, so members can recieve payments, run ecommerce businesses and manage their calendars within and outside the network.

While members can choose whether their activity is visible to the broader network, all information and resources exchanged within their Mycelium, Communities, Groups and Organizations is transparent. There is always a conflict resolution specialists on call to aid members, Communities, Groups and Organizations when disputes or traumas arise, thereby assuring that the network stays healthy enough to realize its vision of humanity working together to restore individual, social and ecological healing and growth
